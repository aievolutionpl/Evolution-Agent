# Anti-AI Slop & Visual Humanizer

Use this playbook to stop AI video from looking generically synthetic even when it is technically impressive.

## The anti-slop principle

A good AI video should feel **observed, directed and physically caused**.

It should not feel like the model is constantly proving that it can move the camera, add particles and transform everything.

## Common AI-slop signatures

Reject or reduce:

- arbitrary orbiting camera
- constant floating gimbal movement
- camera motion with no story purpose
- instant teleportation between object states
- random speed ramps
- excessive slow motion
- neon cyberpunk as a default "AI" look
- volumetric fog in every scene
- teal-orange grading without motivation
- excessive lens flare
- particles that do not come from a source
- plastic skin
- perfect symmetrical posing
- frozen faces during movement
- impossible hand-object intersections
- frictionless body motion
- floating feet
- clothing that ignores momentum
- food that stretches like rubber
- products that melt or redesign themselves
- architecture that morphs during camera movement
- subtitles or UI that were never requested
- generic "epic" music unrelated to pacing
- a new camera angle every second
- transitions used only because they look flashy

## Humanizer layer

When humans appear, add only the micro-behaviour appropriate to the scene.

Useful cues:

- subtle breathing
- small weight shift before walking
- natural blink cadence
- eye movement that precedes head movement
- fingers settling after gripping an object
- shoulder movement caused by a reach
- fabric lag after a turn
- hair responding lightly to motion or wind
- tiny posture correction after stopping
- reaction timing that is not instantaneous
- a half-second of stillness before a meaningful action

Do not stack all humanizer cues in one shot.

## Physical causality rule

Every meaningful change should have a cause.

Bad:

```text
The room transforms into a luxury finished room.
```

Better:

```text
A tradesperson passes close to the lens, fully occluding the frame. As the occlusion clears, the same architecture is revealed finished and painted. Wall geometry, fireplace and floor remain unchanged.
```

Bad:

```text
The bottle flies into her hand.
```

Better:

```text
Her right hand enters frame, fingers wrap around the lower third of the bottle, then she lifts it vertically from the table.
```

## Camera motivation test

Before any camera move, answer:

> What does this movement reveal, emphasize or emotionally change?

If the answer is "it looks cinematic", remove or simplify it.

Good reasons:

- reveal scale
- follow action
- expose product detail
- shift power relationship
- move from observation to intimacy
- hide a transition
- create parallax that explains space
- land on the final hero frame

## Human camera imperfection

Cinema is controlled, not robotic.

Depending on style, use:

- nearly-static tripod
- subtle handheld micro-motion
- small operator correction
- believable acceleration / deceleration
- imperfect but intentional follow timing

Do not simulate unstable amateur shake unless requested.

## Performance hierarchy

For human scenes prioritize:

1. eye line
2. body weight
3. contact with environment
4. hand logic
5. facial micro-expression
6. dialogue sync
7. camera polish

If the performance is fake, better lighting will not save the scene.

## Natural environment behaviour

Add environmental movement only when a source exists.

Examples:

- curtain moves because window is open
- hair moves because wind exists
- steam rises from hot food
- reflections change because camera or light moves
- dust appears because something hits the surface
- water responds to contact or gravity

No "cinematic particles" without a cause.

## Commercial anti-slop

Ads need restraint.

Avoid:

- over-transforming the product
- changing packaging for style
- hiding the product behind effects
- putting the CTA inside the generative video when precise typography matters
- adding fake claims or product behaviours
- replacing a service demonstration with abstract VFX

The product or service should remain understandable without the effects.

## Social anti-slop

Fast does not mean chaotic.

For short-form:

- hook fast
- cut only when information changes
- use 3–5 meaningful beats for most 8–15 second clips
- maintain a clear subject
- avoid "montage soup"
- let the payoff breathe for a moment

## Visual humanizer checklist

Before final output ask:

- Does gravity exist?
- Do feet contact the ground?
- Do hands touch objects in a believable order?
- Does clothing respond to movement?
- Does camera movement have inertia?
- Does the face behave like a living person?
- Does the environment respond to causes?
- Does the subject pause or settle naturally?
- Is there any effect that exists only because "AI videos do that"?

If the final question is yes, remove it unless it serves the concept.

## Anti-slop final rule

**Specific causality beats decorative spectacle.**
