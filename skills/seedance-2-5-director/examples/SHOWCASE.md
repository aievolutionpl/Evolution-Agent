# 1. Cinematic transformation — human enters a game world

```text
TITLE
Crossing Into The Game

FORMAT
9:16 vertical, 10 seconds, realistic live-action transitioning into stylized game-world realism.

REFERENCE AUTHORITY
@Image1 = exact face identity, hairstyle and body proportions of the subject.
@Image2 = starting real-world environment only.
@Image3 = target game-world environment and visual language only.

CONTINUITY LOCKS
Preserve exact face and hairstyle throughout the transition. The same person must remain recognizable before and after the environment changes. No duplicate subject.

0–2s — HOOK
Medium rear three-quarter shot as the subject approaches a glowing rectangular portal embedded naturally in the real environment. Camera tracks slowly behind at waist height with a 35mm lens feel. Portal light spills physically onto clothing and nearby surfaces.
End state: subject stands one step from the portal; face briefly visible in side profile.

2–4.5s — CROSSING
The subject steps through with the right foot first. Camera follows continuously through the portal rather than cutting. As the camera crosses the boundary, the architecture around him transforms progressively from real-world materials into the target game environment. The change travels from background toward foreground, not instant teleportation.
End state: both feet are inside the game world; body and face remain unchanged.

4.5–7s — SCALE REVEAL
Cut to a wide low-angle shot. The subject looks up as a huge stylized game landscape opens around him. Camera performs a slow crane-up while keeping him in the lower third.
End state: the new world is clearly established and the subject remains the visual anchor.

7–10s — PAYOFF
A recognizable in-world transport object passes overhead. The subject takes two steps forward and looks toward it. Camera moves into a medium frontal tracking shot and settles on his reaction.
End state: subject faces camera in the game environment, stable identity, motion settles on a clean hero frame.

AUDIO
Real-world room ambience at start, low portal energy build, brief spatial transition sound as the camera crosses, then open atmospheric game-world wind and distant vehicle rumble. Music rises only after the crossing.

CONSTRAINTS
No face drift. No duplicated subject. No instant environment teleportation. No random UI or text. No camera clipping through the body. Preserve physically plausible footsteps and ground contact.
```

# 2. Luxury perfume commercial

```text
AD OBJECTIVE
Create desire by making the perfume feel tactile, precise and expensive.

FORMAT
9:16 vertical, 10 seconds, premium live-action commercial.

REFERENCE AUTHORITY
@Image1 = exact perfume bottle geometry, glass colour, cap, label position and liquid level.
@Image2 = dark marble bathroom architecture and material palette only.

PRODUCT LOCKS
Preserve exact bottle proportions and label placement throughout. No redesign, no duplicate bottle, no warped glass.

0–2.5s — DISCOVERY
Macro shot through one foreground water droplet on glass. Focus racks from the droplet to the perfume bottle standing on wet dark marble. Narrow warm rim light traces the bottle edges while the room remains subdued.
End state: bottle silhouette and cap are clearly readable.

2.5–5s — MATERIAL DESIRE
Extreme close-up of condensation sliding down the bottle. Camera performs a restrained lateral macro slider move. Highlights travel predictably across the glass.
End state: glass texture and liquid colour are visible without distortion.

5–7.5s — INTERACTION
A clean human hand enters from camera-right and grips the bottle naturally around the lower third, fingers remaining outside the label area. Camera tracks upward a few centimetres with the bottle.
End state: product remains fully intact in the hand.

7.5–10s — HERO
Cut to a stable 85mm-feel beauty composition. Bottle rests again on marble. Very slow micro push-in, warm practical bokeh behind it, crisp edge light, clean negative space in upper third for campaign copy.
End state: motion fully settled on a campaign-ready hero frame.

AUDIO
Quiet luxury ambience, one delicate glass-contact sound, restrained electronic pulse, subtle final low-frequency accent.

CONSTRAINTS
No random text. No extra hands. No melting glass. No product redesign. No impossible reflections. No camera clipping.
```

# 3. Home renovation / fireplace service ad

```text
AD OBJECTIVE
Show that one team can take a fireplace area from unfinished installation to a complete polished room.

FORMAT
9:16 vertical, 12 seconds, realistic local-service commercial.

REFERENCE AUTHORITY
@Image1 = exact room architecture and fireplace placement before finishing.
@Image2 = target finished wall colour and final interior styling only.

ENVIRONMENT LOCKS
Preserve wall positions, fireplace opening, chimney breast, windows, floor and room dimensions. Do not redesign the fireplace.

0–3s — PROBLEM
Wide shot of the unfinished fireplace wall. Plaster edges and unfinished surface are clearly visible. Camera performs a slow lateral reveal from behind a doorway edge.
End state: viewer clearly understands the unfinished state.

3–6s — CRAFT
Medium shot. A tradesperson applies plaster smoothly to the wall beside the fireplace using a real trowel. Tool remains in physical contact with the wall. No jumpy time-lapse morphing.
End state: one visible section is smoothly finished.

6–9s — TRANSFORMATION
Use a motivated occlusion transition as the tradesperson passes close to camera. When the frame clears, the wall is fully finished and painted in the target colour. Architecture remains identical.
End state: construction work is complete and tools are removed.

9–12s — FINISHED ROOM HERO
Clean wide three-quarter view of the finished fireplace area. Fire burns naturally behind the closed glass door. Warm room practicals and soft daylight create balanced contrast. Slow controlled push-in.
End state: portfolio-ready finished room, no debris, no workers, architecture unchanged.

AUDIO
Natural room tone, light plastering tool sound during craft beat, subtle transition whoosh hidden by occlusion, quiet fireplace crackle in final shot.

CONSTRAINTS
No changing wall geometry. No floating tools. No worker passing objects through closed fireplace glass. No random text. Preserve exact fireplace design and architecture.
```

# 4. Premium burger ad

```text
AD OBJECTIVE
Make the burger look hot, fresh and structurally believable.

FORMAT
9:16 vertical, 10 seconds, realistic premium food commercial.

REFERENCE AUTHORITY
@Image1 = exact burger build, bun shape, patty thickness and ingredient order.

FOOD LOCKS
Use the same burger throughout. Preserve ingredient count and order. No duplicated toppings.

0–2s — TEXTURE HOOK
Extreme macro of the grilled patty edge. Tiny beads of juice catch warm side light. Camera slides laterally a few centimetres. Surface texture remains crisp and realistic.
End state: meat texture is clearly readable.

2–5s — REVEAL
Three-quarter macro shot. A hand gently lifts the top bun, revealing patty and toppings without stretching or floating ingredients. Subtle hot steam rises from the meat.
End state: burger interior structure is clearly visible.

5–8s — PAYOFF
A clean chef's knife cuts through the burger once. Camera remains nearly static to preserve geometry. Sauce compresses and moves with realistic viscosity rather than exploding outward.
End state: cross-section is open and clearly readable.

8–10s — HERO
Whole burger on a clean dark surface. Slow micro push-in, warm soft key from camera-left, crisp rim on bun and meat, restrained background bokeh, clear upper-third copy space.
End state: stable campaign hero frame.

AUDIO
Grill sizzle, subtle bun contact, clean knife-cut sound, restrained low commercial music bed.

CONSTRAINTS
No floating ingredients. No impossible cheese stretch. No warped fingers. No burger duplication. No random text. Preserve exact food build.
```

# 5. Luxury fashion editorial

Minimal editorial movement that makes the person and garment feel premium rather than over-produced.

FORMAT
9:16 vertical, 10 seconds, realistic fashion-film aesthetic.

REFERENCE AUTHORITY

@Image3 = architectural environment only.

CONTINUITY LOCKS
Preserve exact face, garment design, jewellery and body proportions throughout.

0–3s — SILHOUETTE
Medium-long shot. Subject steps from shadow into a large soft pool of side light. Camera stays mostly static with an 85mm lens feel, allowing the silhouette to become readable.
End state: full outfit silhouette is visible.

Medium close-up as the subject turns slowly. Fabric responds naturally to movement. Camera performs a restrained side track of less than one metre. Focus stays on face, then gently shifts to the garment detail for one second.

Close portrait. Subject looks slightly off-camera, then turns eyes toward lens. Large soft key, subtle negative fill, natural skin texture.

8–10s — HERO

End state: magazine-style final frame.

CONSTRAINTS
No face drift. No garment redesign. No plastic skin. No duplicated jewellery. No random wind unless physically present.

# 6. Automotive commercial

```text
AD OBJECTIVE
Show vehicle design and controlled performance without losing body accuracy.

FORMAT
16:9, 12 seconds, realistic cinematic automotive commercial.

REFERENCE AUTHORITY
@Image1 = exact car body design, paint colour, lights, badge and wheel geometry.
@Image2 = mountain road environment only.

VEHICLE LOCKS
Preserve exact body proportions, wheels and lighting signature. Tyres remain in physical contact with the road.

0–3s — DESIGN HOOK
Low macro tracking shot along front wheel arch and headlight as the car stands still. Controlled reflections travel along the paint.
End state: body design is clearly established.

3–7s — MOTION
Low side tracking shot at vehicle speed. Camera remains parallel to the car with stable horizon. Wheels rotate naturally and suspension responds subtly to the road.
End state: full side profile remains readable during motion.

7–10s — SCALE
Wide 28–35mm-feel shot as the car moves through a long mountain bend. Camera pans smoothly with the vehicle rather than teleporting positions.
End state: location and performance are both visible.

10–12s — HERO
Three-quarter front composition as the vehicle slows into a scenic turnout. Golden low sun creates restrained edge light. Camera makes a subtle forward move.
End state: car fully visible, wheels straight, motion settled, clean campaign frame.

AUDIO
Realistic tyre/road sound, restrained engine note matched to speed, natural wind and distant environment, minimal cinematic music accent.

CONSTRAINTS
No warped wheels. No changing body shape. No floating car. No duplicate vehicle. No impossible reflections. No uncontrolled drifting.
```
