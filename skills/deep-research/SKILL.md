# Deep Research

## Purpose
Use this skill when the user needs strategic, data-backed answers (market sizing, competitor review, trend mapping, due diligence).

## Output Contract
- Executive summary (5-8 bullets)
- Evidence table with sources, dates, and confidence
- Opportunities / risks
- Recommended next actions
